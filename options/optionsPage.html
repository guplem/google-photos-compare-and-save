Google Photos Compare & Save

Instant swap

Removes the slide between photos so you can flick back and forth and compare them.

The last two settings apply from the next page load. Reload Google Photos after changing them.

Unsaved badges

Marks each album thumbnail that is not in your own library yet. Open an album and press Scan album in the panel at the bottom left to fill in the marks.

Scan speed. Lower numbers scan faster. Raise them if the scan reports photos it could not read.

Button names

The extension decides whether a photo is saved by reading the viewer toolbar. Put one button name per line, in your own language. Use Copy diagnostics in the panel to see the names Google Photos actually shows you.

Stored data

Scan results stay on this computer, in the extension's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
